Buying in Turkey, step by step

The essentials every Libyan and international buyer should know before purchasing in Istanbul.

Good to know

This guide is general information, not legal advice. Rules can change, so every purchase should be confirmed with the licensed agency and an independent lawyer before you pay.

Ask us a question
Can Libyan citizens buy property in Turkey?

Yes. Since Turkey changed its law in 2012, Libyan citizens and most other foreign nationals can own property in their own name, with the same freehold rights as Turkish owners. Some areas, such as military and security zones, are closed to all foreign buyers.

What is the Tapu?

The Tapu is the official title deed, registered at the Turkish Land Registry. When you buy, the property is transferred to you at the Land Registry office and the Tapu is issued in your name. It is the only document that proves ownership.

Can buying property lead to Turkish citizenship?

Yes. Buying property worth at least US$400,000, confirmed by a licensed valuation report, can qualify you for the citizenship-by-investment route. You must commit not to sell the property for three years, and this commitment is noted on the Tapu. Every property in our $1M to $3M range is well above the threshold.

Who handles the paperwork?

The licensed real estate agency handles the purchase process and the transfer at the Land Registry. Turkly introduces you to the right property and agency and stays with you throughout. We also recommend having your own independent lawyer review the purchase.

Do I need to be in Istanbul to buy?

Many buyers visit to see the property and sign in person. It is also possible to complete a purchase through a lawyer with power of attorney. Ask us and we will explain which option fits your situation.

How do I pay safely?

Payments should go through the banking system, not in cash, so there is a clear record that matches the price declared on the Tapu. A clear payment trail protects you and is required for the citizenship route. Turkly never handles your purchase money.
